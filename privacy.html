Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Chintu Chaos channel and the Chintu Chaos Uploader, the tool that publishes our videos.

What the uploader does

The Chintu Chaos Uploader uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the Chintu Chaos YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner. It asks for permission to upload videos and nothing else.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data from viewers. The uploader keeps one access credential for the channel owner's own account, stored as an encrypted secret, so it can upload videos. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data.

Use of Google data

The Chintu Chaos Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Removing access

The account owner can revoke the uploader's access at any time from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
